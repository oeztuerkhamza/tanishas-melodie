1. Collection and storage of personal data, and how it is used

a) Getting in touch

If you contact me by contact form, email or WhatsApp, the following data is processed: name, email address, phone number, event details and the text of your message. This is done to handle your enquiry under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures).

The form on this page opens your email program with the finished message. It is only transmitted when you send it there yourself.

b) Performing a contract

When a contract is concluded, I process your first and last name, address, contact details, event place and date, and contract and invoice data. This is done to perform the contract under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

c) Invoicing and retention

Invoice data is stored for 10 years because of statutory retention duties under German law (HGB, AO).

d) Communication via WhatsApp

If you wish to communicate via WhatsApp, this is voluntary. WhatsApp is a service of Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.; a transfer of data to third countries cannot be ruled out. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

2. Sharing data

Your personal data is only passed to third parties where this is necessary to perform the contract or where there is a legal obligation.

3. Storage period

Personal data is only stored for as long as is needed for the purposes concerned or as statutory retention periods require.

4. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing. You can withdraw consent at any time. Complaints can be lodged with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

5. Hosting

This page is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver and secure the page. A transfer to the USA is possible. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation).

6. Cookies, analytics and fonts

This page sets no cookies and uses no analytics tools. Fonts and scripts are loaded from the same address as the page itself.

7. YouTube videos

The song samples are only loaded when you click play. Data such as your IP address is then sent to YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), and a transfer to the USA is possible. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by clicking.

8. Links to other sites

This page links to Instagram and YouTube, among others. When you follow these links, the privacy policies of those providers apply.
