Four bars to your song

  1. Bar 1

    You write to me

    The date, the place and a few lines about your day are enough. I’ll reply as soon as I can.

  2. Bar 2

    We get to know each other

    Over a coffee or on a video call. We should be in harmony, and a familiar face takes some of the nerves away on the big day.

  3. Bar 3

    We choose your songs

    To suit you and the style of the day: relaxed for a celebrant ceremony, traditional in church or solemn at the registry office.

  4. Bar 4

    I sing for you

    I bring my own microphone and speaker, tuned to my music. It works just as well in a hall as in a meadow with no power supply.

I know how your day feels.

I’m 26 and live in Freiburg with my husband and our cat. I love cooking and travelling, I’m a huge Taylor Swift fan, and I can think of little more beautiful than weddings. It all began at the ceremonies of couples we were friends with. Singing for friends became my passion.

2024 was the year I married my best friend, after almost eight years together.

So I know the stress and all the decisions well. When we later watched our wedding video, the tears came at exactly the point where my friends sang for us. I wish you that same feeling. That’s why we should be a good fit, and why you should be glad to see me on the day itself.

At the ceremony, your story takes centre stage.

Your wishes, your words, your feelings. The music moves people and carries the moment, exactly as feels right to you.

At the drinks reception, everything can get lighter.

Modern, relaxed, with a touch of boho. Not classic love songs, but songs for what’s happening right now: two people have made their choice, and everyone is celebrating with them.
